Unity URP / Unreal Engine

Frame Prediction

Sole Project Owner

2024–2025

Implemented a complete frame prediction algorithm for Unity URP and Unreal Engine’s Forward pipeline, producing results nearly indistinguishable from fully rendered frames. The predicted-frame render loop takes 7–8 ms, including 2–3 ms for prediction, compared with approximately 30 ms for standard rendering. In a static inner-city scene in AOE at 60 FPS, frame power consumption decreased from 43.7 to 37.7 (13.7%), while overall power consumption dropped from 6.44 to 4.76 (26%). This significantly improved rendering performance and efficiency while reducing mobile-device overheating and stuttering.
